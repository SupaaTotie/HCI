01 / FAVORITE GAME

Roblox

Roblox isn't just a single game—it's an expansive, user-generated gaming platform and creation system. It hosts millions of custom-built mini-games spanning every genre imaginable, from intense obstacle courses (obbies) and roleplay simulators to competitive shooters and story-driven adventures.

Why You’re Interested

  • Endless Variety: You never run out of things to do because there are millions of player-made worlds and games to explore.
  • Social Experience: It’s an ideal space to hang out, chat, and team up with friends in casual, fun environments.
  • Creativity & Customization: You enjoy expressing yourself through customizable avatars or trying your hand at designing your own games in Roblox Studio.
02 / FAVORITE GAME

Minecraft

Minecraft is the ultimate open-world sandbox game where everything is made of blocks. Whether exploring vast randomly generated worlds in Survival mode or building grand architectural structures with unlimited resources in Creative mode, it offers total freedom.

Why You’re Interested

  • Total Freedom: There are no fixed rules—you decide whether you want to build, explore, fight bosses, or master Redstone automation.
  • Creative Expression: It gives you a digital canvas to build anything from a humble starter home to a sprawling sci-fi city.
  • Relaxing Yet Rewarding Loop: The cycle of gathering resources, crafting tools, and overcoming survival challenges provides a satisfying sense of progress.
03 / FAVORITE GAME

Valorant

Valorant is a fast-paced, 5v5 tactical first-person shooter that blends precise gunplay with agent abilities. Players choose unique character classes equipped with utility skills (like smoke screens, heals, and flashes) and compete to plant or defuse a device called the Spike.

Why You’re Interested

  • Competitive Thrill: You enjoy high-stakes gameplay where strategy, quick reaction times, and team coordination directly determine victory.
  • Diverse Playstyles: Each Agent brings distinct abilities, allowing you to adapt your playstyle to fit aggressive entry, defensive holding, or team support.
  • Skill Progression: Mastered shooting mechanics and tactical awareness give you a rewarding feeling of continuous personal improvement as you rank up.
